# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 1, 2026

Preamble is operated by **The Blank State**. This Privacy Policy explains what data Preamble collects, how it is used, and how we protect it.

## 1. Our Privacy Commitment

**We do not sell your personal data in any way.**

Preamble is built on trust. We use data only for legitimate product purposes:

- Make the app work reliably
- Improve the product and user experience
- Support AI features
- Manage crashes and failures
- Prevent abuse and maintain security
- Respond to support requests

We are continuously improving Preamble, but we do not treat your personal information as a product to sell, rent, or trade.

## 2. What We Do Not Save

We do **not** save your task content for our own use, including:

- Task titles and descriptions
- Tags, priorities, and dates
- Reminders and subtasks
- Completion status

We also do **not** save your AI chat history as a permanent company record. AI chat content may be processed to answer your requests but is not retained or used to build a profile of you.

## 3. What We May Save

We save information necessary to provide app features you explicitly use:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, Firebase user ID, and sign-in details
- **AI Memory:** Preferences and facts you choose to save for more personalized AI responses
- **App Settings & Preferences:** Your configuration and feature selections
- **Problem Reports:** Text, images, and videos you attach when reporting issues
- **Diagnostic Data:** Device information, app version, diagnostic details, and failure information to fix bugs and improve reliability

**Purpose:** Keep Preamble working reliably, enable support, and improve the product without selling your data.

## 4. Google and Firebase Services

Preamble uses the following Google and Firebase services to provide core app features:

- **Google Sign-In:** Authentication
- **Google Calendar Sync:** Optional integration when you connect your calendar
- **Google Tasks Sync:** Optional integration when you connect Google Tasks
- **Firebase Authentication:** Secure sign-in
- **Firebase Firestore:** Account records, settings, AI memory, problem reports, and operational app data
- **Firebase Cloud Functions:** Server-side app features
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** Push notifications
- **Firebase Storage:** Uploaded files and problem report attachments

**Important:** Google Calendar and Google Tasks integrations are optional and only active when you choose to connect them. These integrations help Preamble work with your workspace. We do not sell or share your Google data with anyone.

## 5. AI Services

Preamble uses third-party AI services to provide the following features you request:

- AI chat and conversations
- Task parsing and extraction
- Natural language processing
- Summaries and suggestions
- AI memory and personalization
- AI-assisted task and reminder behavior

**Data Handling:** When you use AI features, relevant prompts or context may be sent to an AI provider. AI memory is saved only when this feature is enabled or used.

**Important Disclaimer:** AI can make mistakes. Task parsing and natural language processing may be inaccurate. You are responsible for reviewing important tasks, dates, reminders, priorities, and suggestions before relying on them.

## 6. Analytics and Reliability

We use PostHog analytics and Firebase/Firestore operational data to:

- Understand what features are working well
- Identify confusing or problematic areas
- Detect where failures occur
- Manage crashes and failures
- Improve reliability and user experience

**Use:** Analytics are used exclusively for product improvement and crash/failure management. They are **not** used to sell your personal data.

## 7. Advertising

Preamble may display minimal ads to help support AI feature costs.

**Ad Placement:**
- Ads are shown **only** in the AI chat screen
- Ads are **optional and user-initiated** (e.g., rewarded ads for AI credits)
- Ads are **not** displayed elsewhere in the app
- Users can decline ads without losing core functionality

## 8. Problem Reports

When you report a problem, we may collect the following to investigate and resolve issues:

- Problem title and description
- Screenshots and screen recordings
- Device information and app version
- Status history and diagnostic details

**Report Management:**
- Admins review reports and update status (open, in review, resolved)
- Resolved reports and attachments may be deleted by admins
- Reports are used only for app improvement and support

## 9. Data Sharing

**General Policy:** We do not share your personal data with anyone except service providers necessary to operate selected app features.

**Permitted Service Providers:**
- Google/Firebase (authentication, storage, messaging)
- PostHog (analytics)
- Third-party AI providers (AI features)
- Ad services (optional advertising)

**Purpose:** These providers enable Preamble to function, sync data, analyze reliability, provide AI features, or support optional ads. They are **not** used to sell your personal data.

**Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to protect the security and integrity of Preamble.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain only the data necessary for features you actively use:

- Account records and authentication data
- Settings and preferences
- AI memory (when enabled)
- Diagnostic and reliability data
- Problem reports and attachments

**Deletion:**
- Problem report attachments may be deleted after reports are resolved
- Operational records may remain in backups or logs for a limited time
- Unused data is not retained indefinitely

## 11. Security

We implement multiple security measures to protect your data:

- Firebase Authentication for secure access control
- Firestore security rules to restrict data access
- Storage rules for file protection
- Server-side validation and checks
- Industry-standard encryption practices

**Commitment:** While no system is perfect, our goal is to ensure you feel safe using Preamble and to protect your information from unauthorized access.
